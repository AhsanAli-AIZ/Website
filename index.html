How we work

What the first ninety days look like.

No engagement starts with a statement of work. It starts with someone technical actually looking at what you have.

01 / Week 1

Assessment

We audit what is actually running — not what the documentation says is running. You get the findings whether or not you hire us.

02 / Weeks 2–3

Scope & estimate

A written plan with sequencing, dependencies, and a cost range. Anything we are unsure about is flagged as unsure.

03 / Weeks 4–10

Build & migrate

Work lands in reviewable increments. You see progress every two weeks, and you can change direction between them.

04 / Ongoing

Run & support

Handover to your team, or we keep operating it under a support agreement with agreed response targets.
