Partnerships

Selling into the Americas is a distribution problem, not a product problem.

Good technology fails in new markets for reasons that have nothing to do with the technology — no local references, no procurement relationships, no one who can answer a question in the buyer's time zone. That is the gap we fill.

What we do

A route to market that already has relationships in it.

AIZ Pro acts as a business development, sales, and strategic advisory partner for technology vendors expanding into the United States, Canada, and Latin America. Our founder has spent close to three decades building exactly these channels — that experience is the substance of what we bring.

We are selective. We take on vendors whose products we would recommend on their merits — our reputation with buyers is what makes an introduction from us worth having.

We will not represent a product we would not deploy for our own clients. That constraint is why our recommendations carry weight.

How we assess every partnership inquiry
What you get

Concretely, what a partnership involves.

Every arrangement is negotiated individually, but these are the components most of them are assembled from.

Market entry planning
An honest read on whether your product fits the region, what has to change about the positioning, and what the realistic timeline looks like.
Business development
Direct introductions into our network across legal, healthcare, finance, construction, logistics, and technology buyers.
Local delivery capability
Our engineers implement and support your product, so buyers are not asked to trust a vendor with no presence in their time zone.
Channel development
Building the reseller and referral relationships that make the market sustainable after the first few direct deals.
Regulatory context
What the compliance expectations actually are in each vertical, and which of your claims will need substantiating.
